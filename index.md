# Accounting & Financial Statements Study Guide

A practical, no-fluff guide to reading the three financial statements — built for investment-banking and markets interview prep, and for self-study. It assumes you are analytically strong (CS, stats, econ, or quant finance) and have never taken an accounting course, so it teaches **why a number moved** rather than how to post a journal entry.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic in the left nav is a **standalone page you can read in ~10 minutes**.
- Every page follows the same shape: **why it matters**, **core concepts**, a **mental model**, **interview questions** with brief answer keys, and a short **watch list** of verified YouTube videos.
- Topics run foundational → applied. Read them in order the first time, or jump straight to the topic you need.
- Interview badges tell you where to spend prep time: **🎯 Frequent**, **Occasional**, or **Background**.
- Nothing here requires a backend, a data feed, an API key, or a login — it's just reading.

## Suggested path for a banking screen

If you have two hours: [01](topics/01-accounting-equation/) → [02](topics/02-accrual-vs-cash/) → [04](topics/04-income-statement/) → [05](topics/05-balance-sheet/) → [06](topics/06-cash-flow-statement/) → [07](topics/07-linking-three-statements/), then rehearse [14](topics/14-interview-hotspots/) out loud until it's smooth.

## Scope

**In scope**: the accounting equation, accrual accounting, the three statements, how they link, revenue recognition, expense matching, working capital, ratios, D&A, inventory/COGS, and interview delivery.

**Out of scope** (by design): CPA exam prep, tax accounting, consolidations and M&A purchase accounting, and anything requiring a login.
